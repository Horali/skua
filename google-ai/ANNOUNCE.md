## Short announcement

> **ePlus AI Toolkit**
>
> A Windows-focused Textual terminal dashboard and PowerShell/Python toolkit for inspecting Google AI Pro offer information on a rooted Android environment, such as MuMuPlayer, using ADB and Frida.
>
> Includes read-only offer listing, environment diagnostics, optional advanced checkout steps, offline regression tests, and guides in English, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish and French.
>
> **Important:** Steps 2–5 may initiate real Google Play charges. A listed offer or a price of zero does not prove eligibility, activation or renewal. Verify all terms directly in Google Play.
>
> Repository: https://github.com/hoangsvit/google-ai
> Published versions: https://github.com/hoangsvit/google-ai/releases

## Technical announcement

> The Textual dashboard invokes the existing PowerShell/Python engine; it does not implement a second billing engine. The project preserves upstream Android/Frida components and adds Windows/MuMu setup diagnostics, explicit TUI approvals and mocked CI tests.
>
> Run `run-all.ps1 -Step 1` to list offers without opening a subscription checkout. Environment preparation can still install or modify ADB/Frida/emulator settings. Checkout Steps 2–5 remain potentially billable; standalone CLI stages differ from TUI approval handling.
>
> Historical 12-month/+6-month stage names do not guarantee subscription extensions. The toolkit cannot independently verify a completed purchase or actual renewal date.
